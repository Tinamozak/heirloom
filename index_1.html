Settings

Turns on the ✨ AI Guess button — it looks at your photo and suggests a name, category and ballpark value. Your key is stored only on this device, in this browser, and is sent only to Anthropic. Leave it blank and everything else still works.
Your home is private. Everything you log — photos, values, voice memories — is stored only on this device and this browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server, there's no account, and there's no tracking. The one exception is the optional ✨ AI Guess, which sends just that one photo to Anthropic over an encrypted connection when you tap it. A passcode adds a lock over the app on this device; for a play-MVP that's the right level of protection — bank-grade encryption and heir accounts come in the real build.
Your inventory lives on this device only. Add this page to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) to keep it one tap away. Export a CSV anytime to move it into a spreadsheet.
